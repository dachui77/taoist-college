學院概況與日常 College Overview & Daily Affairs

辦學宗旨 Our Mission

我們致力於弘揚道教開創的智慧，將古老的哲學傳統與現代高等教育深度結合。依託清幽寧靜的道場環境，三清宮不僅營造了一處隔絕喧囂的寧靜境地，更建立起系統嚴謹的學術研習機制，輔助學子體悟“道法自然”的真諦。 We are committed to promoting the wisdom of Taoism, deeply integrating ancient philosophical traditions with modern higher education. Relying on our tranquil environment, Sanqing Palace creates a peaceful haven and establishes a rigorous academic study mechanism to help students comprehend the true meaning of "Tao follows nature".

招生與修學體系 Admissions & Study System

青年學生（學歷與長期研習） Young Students (Degree & Long-term Study)

學制 (Duration)： 2年制 / 4年制 (2-year / 4-year)

核心課程 (Core Courses)： 宗教學導論、道教史、科儀規範與演練、早期道家哲學文本精讀。 Introduction to Religious Studies, History of Taoism, Ritual Norms and Practice, Intensive Reading of Early Taoist Philosophical Texts.

培養目標 (Objectives)： 培養具備紮實學術功底與深厚宗教學養的現代道教學者及教職人員。 To cultivate modern Taoist scholars and clergy with a solid academic foundation and profound religious accomplishment.

社會各界（短期研修與工坊） Social Sectors (Short-term Retreats & Workshops)

項目 (Programs)： 7天止息觀照營 / 週末道醫養生班 / 哲學工坊 7-Day Meditation Camp / Weekend Taoist Medicine Class / Philosophy Workshop

修持內容 (Practices)： 吐納調息、林間經行、經典夜話。暫別電子屏幕與信息雜音，重建身心秩序。 Breathing exercises, forest walking, evening classic talks. Disconnect from screens to rebuild physical and mental order.

受眾 (Audience)： 面向有志於探尋安頓身心路徑的企業管理者及各界人士。 Business managers and individuals from all walks of life interested in exploring paths to settle their body and mind.

學術交流中心 Academic Exchange Center

學院定期舉辦宗教學高峰論壇，並積極與海內外高校哲學系、宗教學系開展互訪交流。致力於將傳統道家思想引入當代生命科學與認知科學的跨學科對話之中。 The college regularly hosts high-level forums on religious studies and actively engages in mutual visits and exchanges with philosophy and religious studies departments at universities home and abroad. We are committed to introducing traditional Taoist thought into interdisciplinary dialogues with contemporary life and cognitive sciences.
